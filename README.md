## <img src="assets/icon.svg" alt="Frame Capture Studio logo" width="32" height="32" align="center"> Frame Capture Studio

Capture sharp, full-resolution still frames from your videos directly in your browser.

Frame Capture Studio is an open-source tool that takes frames from a video at exactly the moments you choose and exports them as PNG or JPG images. Place the cursor where you want to begin, pick how often and how many, and the app does the rest.

All processing happens locally in your browser. Your videos are never uploaded or stored.

🌐 **Live Demo Link :** https://aligokdam.github.io/frame.capture.studio/

---

## Features

- 🎯 Start from the beginning or from any point on the timeline
- ⏱️ Adjustable interval between frames
- 🔢 A set number of frames, or every frame until the video ends
- 🖼️ Frames saved at the video's original resolution
- 📸 Grab the single frame under the cursor at any time
- 🎞️ Filmstrip timeline that marks every frame before you capture it
- 💾 PNG & JPG export, one by one, as a ZIP, or straight into a folder
- 📂 Drag & Drop video upload
- 🌍 Multilingual interface, including right-to-left languages
- 🌓 Light and dark themes
- 🔒 Fully client-side processing

---

## How It Works

1. Upload a video.
2. Choose Whole video, or From cursor and drag the cursor to your starting moment.
3. Set the interval and how many frames to capture.
4. Extract the frames and export them as PNG or JPG.
